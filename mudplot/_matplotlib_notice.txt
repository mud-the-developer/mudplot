Matplotlib date-format policy adaptation
=======================================

LicenseRef-Matplotlib in the distribution License-Expression refers to the
Matplotlib license reproduced below. The project's own LICENSE remains MIT.

The canonical concise formatter in mudplot/_axis_render.py adapts the finite
level/zero/offset/trailing-zero format-selection policy of Matplotlib 3.8.0,
matplotlib.dates.ConciseDateFormatter.format_ticks (dates.py, lines 769-850).
The auto date format table follows Matplotlib 3.8.0's documented defaults.
Source: https://github.com/matplotlib/matplotlib/blob/v3.8.0/lib/matplotlib/dates.py
License: https://matplotlib.org/3.8.0/users/project/license.html

Copyright (c) 2012- Matplotlib Development Team; All Rights Reserved

Summary of changes: date values are converted from mudplot's canonical UTC
1970-day coordinates using datetime/timedelta and the declared fixed-offset
clock, rather than Matplotlib num2date's distant-epoch 20-microsecond rounding.
Caller-supplied format arrays/functions are not exposed; the finite policy is
bounded by mudplot's configured tick/location/text operation limits. A detailed
fixed-tick ISO path retains date and supported microseconds. No global epoch,
Matplotlib module, source observations or global formatter defaults are changed.
